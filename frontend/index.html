      About this tool

      The Dropout-Risk Hotspot Mapper combines school-level structural features with district-level infrastructure deficit to estimate a Demo Risk Score for schools across Karnataka.

      Confidence Flagging: Markers with a dashed outline indicate a "Low" confidence score. This is not a statistical confidence interval, just a documented disagreement flag when a school's local structural signals diverge strongly from its district's broader context.
