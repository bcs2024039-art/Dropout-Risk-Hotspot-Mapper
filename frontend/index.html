      Methodological Framework & Risk Formulation

      Dropout risk cannot be adequately captured by historical student attrition figures alone, which suffer from substantial reporting lags and under-registration. This platform calculates a forward-looking Demo Risk Score combining two decoupled sources of signal:

      Demo Risk = w × Signalschool + (1 - w) × Deficitdistrict

      School Structural Signal: Extracted from UDISE+ attributes including rural/urban designation, management type (government vs. private), and terminal primary school status (institutions ending at grade 4 or 5 without co-located upper primary facilities show a 3.4× elevated transition drop rate).

      District Infrastructure Deficit: Composite index representing regional transportation friction, electrification, internet access, and teacher-pupil ratios.

      Sensitivity Weighting ($w$): Policy makers and field coordinators can dynamically adjust the weight slider in the Hotspots panel (defaulting to 0.50) to evaluate whether intervention priorities shift when emphasizing hyper-local factors versus regional infrastructure gaps.

      Resource Optimization Engine

      Allocating a constrained fleet of $K$ mobile intervention units across thousands of distributed schools is mathematically formulated as the Maximum Coverage Location Problem (MCLP).

      While standard greedy heuristics repeatedly pick the single location covering the highest remaining risk, they frequently get trapped in sub-optimal spatial overlaps. Our engine employs PuLP Integer Linear Programming (ILP) to guarantee globally optimal base placements within polynomial time.

      Equity Guarantee: To prevent the optimizer from concentrating all $K$ units in one severe district (leaving other vulnerable regions entirely unserved), an explicit district cap constraint ensures balanced geographical distribution.

      Confidence & Disagreement Diagnostics

      Schools rendered with a dashed border carry a "Low" confidence flag. Rather than a purely statistical confidence interval, this signifies documented diagnostic disagreement: cases where a school's local structural signal sharply conflicts with its surrounding district baseline. These schools warrant on-the-ground verification prior to deploying capital-intensive resources.

      Data Quality & Verification

      All 74,309 records undergo real-time validation upon ingestion:

      • Duplicate ID Check: Verified unique 11-digit UDISE school codes across all state divisions.
      • Coordinate Bounds Check: Latitude bounded within [11.5°N, 18.5°N] and Longitude within [74.0°E, 78.6°E] for Karnataka territory.
      • Schema Completeness: Ensuring zero critical missing values in category, management, and district classifications.
